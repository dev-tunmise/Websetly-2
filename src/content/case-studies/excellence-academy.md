## The situation

Excellence Academy handled admissions on paper. Prospective families had to
visit in person during office hours to collect and return forms, which quietly
limited the applicant pool to people who could take time off work.

## What we did

- Built a modern, mobile-first site that works on the phones families actually
  own, on the connections they actually have
- Added an online admissions flow that accepts applications and supporting
  documents without a visit
- Built a student portal so current families have one place for the information
  they previously had to phone the office for

## The outcome

Admissions now run online, and the office spends materially less time
re-explaining the process over the phone.
