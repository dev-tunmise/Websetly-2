## The situation

Restaurant sites lose bookings in a predictable way: the menu is a PDF that will
not open properly on a phone, and the reservation link sits three taps from where
the visitor is standing. Lumiere had both problems.

## What we did

- Replaced the PDF menu with a real, readable web page that loads instantly and
  can be found in search
- Moved reservations to the first screen on mobile, where the decision is
  actually being made
- Presented the room and the food at a size that looks considered without
  shipping full-resolution photography to a phone

## The outcome

Diners can read the menu and book a table without pinch-zooming a PDF, and the
menu itself is now indexable content rather than an opaque download.
