## The situation

TechFlow had a capable B2B product wrapped in an interface that made people work
to find what they needed. Engagement was flat, and a large share of visitors left
the dashboard without completing the task they had arrived to do.

## What we did

We reworked the platform around the handful of jobs users actually came to
perform, rather than around the structure of the underlying data model.

- Redesigned the dashboard so the most common actions are reachable immediately
- Simplified the primary user flows and removed steps that existed only for
  internal reasons
- Rebuilt the integration surfaces so connecting other tools stopped being a
  support ticket

## The outcome

User engagement increased by 150% and bounce rate fell by 40%.
