## The situation

Property portals are image-heavy by nature, and MashInc's buyers were browsing
listings on phones over mobile data. Every additional megabyte of gallery imagery
was a buyer who scrolled past the listing instead of opening it.

## What we did

- Built a property search that filters without a full page reload
- Delivered listing galleries in modern image formats, sized for the device
  requesting them rather than the largest possible screen
- Added virtual tours that load on demand instead of on page load, so a visitor
  only pays for the tour they actually open

## The outcome

Listings open quickly on mobile connections, and the gallery weight no longer
scales with the number of photos an agent uploads.
