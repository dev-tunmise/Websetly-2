## Money-Saving Tips for Domain Registration

### 💰 Tip 1: Register for Multiple Years

Lock in current prices by registering for 2-5 years upfront. This protects you from price increases and reduces administrative work.

**Savings:** Up to ₦500/year by avoiding price increases

### 🛡️ Tip 2: Prioritize Free Privacy Protection

Choose registrars that include privacy protection for free. This saves ₦1,500-₦3,000 annually and protects your personal information.

**Whogohost Advantage:** Free privacy protection worth ₦2,000/year

### ⚡ Tip 3: Avoid Registration + Hosting Bundles

Separate domain and hosting purchases give you flexibility and often better prices. You can always point your domain to any hosting provider.

**Warning:** Bundled packages often have higher renewal rates and lock-in contracts

### 📅 Tip 4: Set Calendar Reminders for Renewals

Track renewal dates to avoid auto-renewal at higher rates. Most registrars allow manual renewal at better prices.

**Pro Tip:** Renew 30 days before expiration to avoid rush fees

### 🔍 Tip 5: Read the Fine Print

Always check renewal rates, transfer policies, and additional fees before registering. The cheapest upfront cost often isn't the best long-term value.

**Red Flag:** Providers that don't clearly display renewal rates upfront
