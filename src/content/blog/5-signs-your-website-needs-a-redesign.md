Your website is often one of the first things people see when they look up your business.

If it looks old, is difficult to use or does not work well, people may leave without contacting you.

Sometimes, small changes are enough. Other times, the website needs a redesign.

Here are five signs to look out for.

## 1. Your website looks old

Design trends change, and a website that looked good a few years ago may now look outdated.

Old layouts, poor colours, hard-to-read text and low-quality images can make your business look less professional.

A fresh design can make your website look better and easier to use.

## 2. It does not work well on phones

Many people visit websites using their phones.

If visitors have to zoom in to read text, struggle to click buttons or scroll sideways to see content, your website needs attention.

Your website should work well on phones, tablets and computers.

## 3. It is difficult to find information

Visitors should not have to search everywhere to understand what your business does or find your contact details.

Your services, important information and contact details should be easy to find.

If your website is confusing, it may be time to improve its layout and structure.

## 4. Your website is slow

People do not like waiting for websites to load.

Large images, unnecessary code and other issues can make your website slow.

You can use the [free analyzer on our website](/analyze) to check your site's performance and Core Web Vitals.

If the results show problems, those issues may need to be fixed.

## 5. Your website no longer fits your business

Your business may have changed since your website was first built.

You may now offer new services, sell different products or target a different type of customer.

If your website no longer shows what your business does, it needs an update.

## Does your website need a redesign?

You do not always need to start from scratch. Some websites only need a few changes, while others need a complete redesign.

The important thing is to make sure your website represents your business and works well for your visitors.
