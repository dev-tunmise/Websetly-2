Many businesses use Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp to reach customers. These platforms are useful, but they are not enough on their own.

A website gives your business a place on the internet that you control. People can visit it, learn about your business and find what they need without having to send you a message first.

## Why your business needs a website

**1. People can find you on Google.**

When someone searches for a service you offer, your website can help them find your business. Without one, you may miss people who are looking for what you sell.

**2. People can learn about your business.**

Your website can show what you do, the services you offer, your prices, your location and how to contact you.

People do not have to ask you the same questions before they understand your business.

**3. It helps people trust your business.**

A good website gives people more information about your business. They can see your work, read about your services and know how to reach you.

**4. You have more control.**

Social media platforms can change their rules, reduce your reach or even restrict your account.

Your website gives you a place to share information about your business without depending on those platforms.

## What should your website have?

You do not need a very large website to start.

A simple business website can have:

- A homepage that explains what your business does.
- A page showing your products or services.
- An About page with information about your business.
- A contact page with your phone number, email or WhatsApp link.

You can add more pages as your business grows.

## Does every business need a website?

Not every business needs a large website. What matters is having a website that serves your business and makes it easier for people to find you.

If people are already searching for your products or services online, a website can help them find you and learn more about what you offer.
