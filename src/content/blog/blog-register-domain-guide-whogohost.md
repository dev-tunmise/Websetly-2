## Step 1: Choose Your Perfect Domain Name

Your domain name is your digital address. Here's how to choose the perfect one:

### Domain Name Best Practices

- ✓**Keep it short and memorable:** Aim for 6-14 characters
- ✓**Use your business name:** Build brand recognition
- ✓**Avoid hyphens and numbers:** They're hard to remember and spell
- ✓**Choose the right extension:** .com is most popular, .ng for Nigerian businesses
- ✗**Avoid trademarked names:** This can lead to legal issues

## Step 2: Check Domain Availability

Now let's check if your desired domain is available. Follow these simple steps:

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### ❓ Payment Failed or Declined

**Solutions:**

- Check if your card has sufficient funds
- Ensure your card is enabled for online transactions
- Try a different payment method
- Contact your bank to authorize the transaction
- Use bank transfer as alternative

### ❓ Domain Not Working After Registration

**Solutions:**

- Wait up to 24 hours for full DNS propagation
- Check that you've configured DNS settings correctly
- Verify your hosting provider's name servers
- Clear your browser cache and try again
- Contact Whogohost support for assistance

### ❓ Didn't Receive Confirmation Email

**Solutions:**

- Check your spam/junk folder
- Verify the email address you provided
- Add support@whogohost.com to your contacts
- Request email resend from customer portal
- Contact customer support for manual confirmation
